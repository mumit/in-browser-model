In-Browser Model Lab

Four small apps and games where the AI runs on your own laptop, inside the browser tab. No server, no API keys, and nothing you type or say leaves your machine.

Before you start

  • Use a recent Chrome or Edge on a laptop. Models run on the graphics card through WebGPU, and fall back to the CPU (slower) without it.
  • The first load is a download. Model weights come from Hugging Face and are cached in your browser, so the second visit is fast. The four apps share models where they can.
  • Corporate networks sometimes block huggingface.co or cdn.jsdelivr.net. If a model won't load, try another network.
  • Scores and settings stay in your browser only. Clearing site data resets them, and removes the cached models too.
  • These are experiments. Small models get things wrong, sometimes confidently. That's part of the fun.
Each app is a single HTML file, and each folder has a PROMPT.md that rebuilds it from scratch. Ideas for what to build next are in IDEAS.md.
