Before you start

  • Use a recent Chrome or Edge on a laptop. Models run on the graphics card through WebGPU, and fall back to the CPU (slower) without it.
  • The first load is a download. Model weights come from Hugging Face and are cached in your browser, so the second visit is fast. The apps share models where they can.
  • Corporate networks sometimes block or slow huggingface.co or cdn.jsdelivr.net. If a model stalls, try another network; dropped downloads retry automatically.
  • Scores and settings stay in your browser only. Clearing site data resets them, and removes the cached models too.
  • These are experiments. Small models get things wrong, sometimes confidently. That's part of the fun.
Each app is a single HTML file, and each folder has a PROMPT.md that rebuilds it from scratch. MIT licensed.
