How it works
  • Speak: Kokoro turns the text into speech, with a different voice for each relay.
  • The line: the audio goes through a phone filter (300–3,400 Hz) plus hiss, crackle and, on tin can, dropouts. The noise is seeded, so the same message on the same settings gives the same chain.
  • Hear: Whisper transcribes what came down the line. Misheard words are highlighted.
  • Retell: a small language model (Qwen2.5 0.5B) passes it on in its own words, with greedy decoding.
  • Score: MiniLM embeds the original and each retelling; cosine similarity is the drift chart.
  • The lesson: every step is “pretty good”, but errors compound. It's the same reason long agent pipelines go off the rails.
