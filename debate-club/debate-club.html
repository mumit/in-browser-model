How it works
  • Personas are system prompts. Each turn, a debater sees the motion, its side, and what its opponent just said.
  • Randomness: by default each turn samples at temperature 0.8 (top-k 40), so rematches differ. Tick “Same every time” for greedy decoding.
  • Pipelining: while one side is speaking, the other side is already writing and voicing its reply, so the gaps stay short.
  • The judge is asked for a single letter, and we read the probability it gives to “A” and “B” directly from its output distribution. It's asked twice, with the sides' labels swapped, to cancel out position bias. Both runs are shown.
  • The applause meter measures microphone loudness for 4 seconds per side and averages the loudest moments.
  • Everything runs in this tab. Nothing leaves your machine.
