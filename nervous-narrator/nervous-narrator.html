How it works
  • At every step the model scores all ~150,000 tokens in its vocabulary. A hook captures that distribution before it picks the top one.
  • Top choice confidence is the probability of the token it picked. Spread is 1 ÷ eentropy: high when probability is concentrated, low when it's spread across many options.
  • A word's confidence is its least confident token. Words with similar confidence are spoken together, so the voice changes smoothly.
  • Lower confidence means slower, quieter speech, a pause before the word and, with the Kokoro voice, a pitch and volume wobble.
  • Confidence isn't correctness. Small models can be sure and wrong. That's what the calibration check is for.
  • Everything runs in this tab. Nothing you say or type leaves your machine.
